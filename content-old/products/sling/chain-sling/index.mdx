## The Role of Chain Slings in Industrial Rigging

**Alloy chain slings** are built from **high-strength alloy steel** link chain (typically **Grade 80** or **Grade 100**) with **master links**, **connectors**, and **hooks** assembled to a **rated working load limit (WLL)**. They are heavier than web or roundslings but excel in **abrasion**, **cut**, and many **hot** or **chemical** environments where fibres degrade quickly.

Design and testing are governed by **national/regional standards**—examples cited in industry literature include **IS 2760**, **EN 818-4**, and **ASME** rigging volumes. **Minimum design factors** (often on the order of **~5:1** between **minimum breaking load** and **WLL** for chain components) and **proof loading** of finished slings (commonly **200% of WLL** in **proof-test** procedures—confirm wording on your **certificate**) are part of qualified manufacture.

## Configurations, Sizes, and Components

* **Legs:** **1–4** leg assemblies are standard; each **leg angle** and **hook** pattern affects the **chart**.
* **Link size:** Catalogue **diameters** often span roughly **6–32 mm** and beyond on large **quarry/mill** rigging; **imperial** sizes (e.g. **5/16 in**, **3/8 in**, **1/2 in**) appear in parallel with **metric** listings.
* **WLL bands:** Tables can run from about **~1 t** class on small **single-leg** builds to **100 t+** territory on **heavy multi-leg** systems—**only** the **tag** and **manufacturer table** for **your** sling geometry are valid.
* **Top fittings:** **Oblong** master links, **pear** links, or **round** rings.
* **Bottom fittings:** **Hook** types with **safety latches**, **self-locking** hooks, **grab** hooks for **shortening**, or **foundry** hooks where **shape** demands.
* **Length:** Length tolerance is often specified (e.g. **−0 / +2** pitch links); **multi-leg** sets may require **matched** leg lengths (e.g. within **~6 mm** on **~2 m** assemblies per supplier rules).

## Performance in Harsh Environments

* **Temperature:** Alloy chain tolerates **hot** conditions better than **synthetic** slings, but **WLL** may require **derating** above **~200–400 °C** class ranges—follow **OEM** limits.
* **Chemistry / UV:** **Steel** resists many solvents and UV better than **polyester** or **nylon**; **corrosion** still limits life—inspect **especially** in **washdown** or **outdoor** stores.
* **Cut / edge:** Chains can **wrap** **rough** loads more readily than **web**, but **nicks** and **gross** **deformation** still condemn links.

## Hitch Effects and Design Factors

* **Choke:** A **choke** hitch typically **reduces** WLL versus a **straight** lift—**~20%** is a **common** rule of thumb in table footnotes; use the **certified** chart.
* **Leg angle:** For **basket** or **multi-leg** picks, **horizontal** **spread** increases **tension** in each leg—use **angle** columns (**30°**, **45°**, **60°** from **vertical** in many tables) **exactly** as printed.
* **Proof and discard:** **Proof** tests and **periodic** **re-inspection** are **not** optional—follow **site** **Lifting Plan** and **local** law.

## Structural Integrity and Inspections

Unlike **wire rope**, where **internal** **corrosion** can hide, **chain** links are **surface-visible**. Inspect **every** link for **stretch**, **gouges**, **cracks**, **nicked** **welds**, and **hook** **latch** function. **Remove** **slings** that fail **criteria** in **ISO 7597 / EN 818-7**-style **inspection** guides or your **owner’s** **procedure**.

## Practical Selection Guidelines

* **Grade:** Use only **Grade 80/100/120** **lifting** **system** **components**—**never** **transport** or **binder** chain for **overhead** lift.
* **Weight:** Chain is **heavy**—plan **handling**, **staging**, and **crane** **capacity** including **sling** **self-weight**.
* **Tag:** Ensure **metal** **tag** with **maker**, **grade**, **size**, **length**, **WLL**, and **traceability** to **test**.

Request **Jayco’s** **rated** **chart**, **assembly** **drawing**, and **certificate** for the **exact** **leg** count, **length**, and **hook** set you order.
