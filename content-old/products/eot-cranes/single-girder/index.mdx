## Single Girder EOT Crane

A Single Girder EOT Crane is one of the most widely used overhead material handling systems for light to medium-duty industrial operations. It is designed with a single bridge girder that travels on runway rails, while the hoist trolley moves along the girder to lift and transfer materials across the work area.

This crane configuration is widely preferred where efficient lifting is required without adding unnecessary structural load to the building. Its lighter construction, lower dead weight, and practical operating range make it a cost-effective solution for workshops, warehouses, production units, and general manufacturing facilities.

## Construction and Working Principle

The crane consists of a single bridge girder supported by end carriages, with an electric wire rope hoist or trolley arrangement mounted for hoisting and cross travel motion. The full bridge travels longitudinally on the runway, allowing load movement in three directions:

- **Hoisting Motion:** vertical lifting and lowering of the load  
- **Cross Travel Motion:** lateral movement of the hoist across the girder  
- **Long Travel Motion:** movement of the crane bridge along the runway  

This three-axis movement enables smooth and controlled handling of materials across a defined bay or shop floor.

## Key Product Advantages

### Lightweight and Space-Efficient Design

A single girder crane uses less structural steel than a double girder configuration, resulting in a lighter overall system. This helps reduce wheel loads and building load requirements while improving the use of available floor space.

### Cost-Effective Material Handling

For many industrial applications, a single girder EOT crane offers an ideal balance between performance and cost. It provides dependable lifting capability for routine handling operations without the additional complexity of heavier crane systems.

### Suitable for Diverse Industrial Applications

Single girder cranes are commonly used in:

- fabrication shops  
- engineering units  
- warehouses  
- machine shops  
- assembly lines  
- maintenance bays  
- general manufacturing facilities  

They are especially suitable where regular lifting is required but the duty cycle and capacity do not justify a double girder system.

## Typical Technical Specifications

The final crane specification depends on load, span, duty cycle, operating environment, and building constraints. Common configuration parameters include:

- **Safe Working Load:** typically from 0.5 ton up to 20 ton  
- **Span:** generally from 5 m to 40 m  
- **Height of Lift:** selected as per shed height and process requirements  
- **Duty Classification:** selected based on usage frequency and load pattern  
- **Hoist Type:** electric wire rope hoist  
- **Control System:** floor-mounted pendant push button, with optional radio remote  
- **Drive System:** motorized hoisting, cross travel, and long travel motions  
- **Speed Selection:** configured based on application, duty, and operational control requirements  

## Standard Technical Features

### Hoisting Unit

Single girder EOT cranes are generally equipped with an electric wire rope hoist designed for dependable lifting performance and smooth operation. The hoist is selected based on required capacity, lift height, duty cycle, and frequency of use.

### Motors

Crane-duty squirrel cage induction motors are typically used for hoisting, cross travel, and long travel motions. These motors are selected for repeated starting, reversing, and braking conditions commonly encountered in industrial crane applications.

### Brakes

Electromagnetic or fail-safe braking systems are used to ensure controlled stopping and safer handling during operation, including response during power failure or emergency stopping conditions.

### Gear Transmission

The drive arrangement is generally designed for smooth power transmission and controlled motion. Gear systems are selected to support reliable torque transfer, stable movement, and long operating life under industrial duty conditions.

### Electrical Panel

The crane electrical system is designed for operational control, motor protection, and safe power distribution. Typical provisions may include overload protection, control circuitry, and operator-friendly maintenance access.

### Limit Switches

Safety limit switches are an important part of the crane system. These are commonly provided for:

- upper hook travel limitation  
- lower hook travel limitation  
- over-travel protection in crane or trolley motion where required  

These features help reduce the risk of over-hoisting, over-lowering, and travel-end collision.

## Optional Features

Depending on site conditions and customer requirements, a Single Girder EOT Crane may also be supplied with optional features such as:

- **Radio Remote Control** for wireless operation  
- **Variable Frequency Drive (VFD / VVVF Drive)** for smoother acceleration and deceleration  
- **Soft Starter** for controlled motor starting  
- **Shrouded Power Supply System / DSL** for current collection  
- **Special electrical protection or flameproof execution** for selected environments  
- **Customized speed combinations** based on duty and process needs  

## Design and Safety Considerations

A properly selected single girder crane should be designed by considering:

- rated load and load spectrum  
- crane span and hook approach  
- building dimensions and headroom  
- required lift height  
- duty cycle and frequency of operation  
- operator control preference  
- environmental conditions inside the plant  

The crane should also incorporate appropriate safety provisions, including controlled hook travel, braking reliability, and electrical protection systems suited to the working environment.

## Standards and Engineering Considerations

Single girder EOT cranes are generally engineered with reference to applicable Indian standards for crane design, safety, and performance. For industrial buyers, compliance-related design considerations are important for structural reliability, operational safety, and long-term service performance.

Applicable standards and design references may include relevant provisions for crane structure, duty classification, hoisting arrangement, safety clearances, and testing requirements.

## Applications of Single Girder EOT Cranes

Single girder EOT cranes are widely used for handling raw materials, components, fabricated assemblies, machine parts, tools, dies, and finished goods. These cranes are suitable for:

- loading and unloading operations  
- intra-bay material transfer  
- machine feeding  
- fabrication handling  
- warehouse lifting operations  
- routine shop-floor movement of industrial loads  

## Why Choose a Single Girder EOT Crane

A single girder EOT crane is a practical choice when the application requires:

- economical overhead lifting  
- compact crane construction  
- reduced structural loading on the building  
- reliable handling for light to medium-duty operations  
- flexibility in control and operating features  

For industrial users looking for an efficient and versatile overhead crane system, the single girder configuration remains one of the most proven and widely adopted solutions.
