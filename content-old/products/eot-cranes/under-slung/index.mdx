## Underslung EOT Crane

An Underslung EOT Crane is a suspended overhead crane system designed for facilities where conventional top-running EOT crane arrangements may not be suitable due to space limitations or structural constraints. In this design, the crane travels beneath the supporting runway structure, making it a practical solution for efficient overhead material handling in compact industrial environments.

Underslung cranes are commonly used for lifting and shifting materials from one location to another where floor space must be utilized efficiently and the installation of a standard overhead traveling crane is not practical.

## Construction and Working Principle

The underslung crane is designed to run below the runway support structure, with the hoist and trolley arrangement suspended from the bridge. This arrangement allows overhead lifting without requiring the same type of space and support layout typically associated with conventional EOT cranes.

The crane typically operates in three controlled motions:

- **Hoisting Motion:** lifting and lowering of the load  
- **Cross Travel Motion:** movement of the hoist along the bridge  
- **Long Travel Motion:** movement of the crane along the suspended runway  

This makes the underslung crane suitable for controlled movement of materials in workshops, small production units, and other industrial applications with restricted space.

## Key Product Advantages

### Space-Efficient Crane Arrangement

The main advantage of an underslung crane is its suitability for locations where there is limited room for a conventional EOT crane setup. Its suspended arrangement helps utilize available space more effectively while still enabling overhead material movement.

### Compact and Lightweight Design

Underslung cranes are commonly preferred for their compact profile and lighter structural arrangement. This makes them suitable for facilities where a smaller and more adaptable material handling system is required.

### Practical for Light to Medium-Duty Applications

These cranes are generally selected for lighter lifting operations and routine material transfer where efficient handling is needed without the complexity of heavier overhead crane systems.

## Typical Technical Specifications

The actual crane configuration depends on the application, layout, and load handling requirement. Common technical parameters include:

- **Safe Working Load:** typically from 0.5 ton up to 20 ton  
- **Span:** generally from 5 m to 20 m  
- **Height of Lift:** selected as per process and building requirement  
- **Hoist Type:** electric wire rope hoist  
- **Operating Mode:** pendant push button or selected as per application  
- **Drive Speeds:** available in single speed or dual speed arrangements  
- **Application Type:** suitable for industrial and light material handling operations  

## Standard Technical Features

### Structural Design

Underslung cranes are designed with a compact suspended structure intended to perform reliable material handling in constrained floor layouts. The design is selected according to load, span, available support arrangement, and application needs.

### Hoisting Arrangement

These cranes are typically equipped with an electric wire rope hoist for lifting and lowering operations. The hoist selection depends on capacity, lift requirement, duty cycle, and operating conditions.

### Motors

The crane uses application-suitable electric motors selected for hoisting and travel motions. Motor configuration may vary depending on speed requirement, duty condition, and operating cycle.

### Brakes

Electromagnetic braking systems are commonly used to provide automatic braking support during operation and in the event of sudden power failure, helping improve operational safety and motion control.

### Gear Transmission

The drive arrangement is designed to provide stable and reliable power transmission for hoisting and travel motions, with suitable gearbox selection based on load and duty conditions.

### Electrical Panel

The electrical panel supports crane control and protection functions, including power distribution and safeguarding of internal components through suitable protective devices.

### Limit Switches

Safety limit switches are commonly provided to control travel and hook movement. Typical safety provisions include:

- upper hook travel limitation  
- lower hook travel limitation  
- end travel limitation where required  

These help reduce the risk of over-hoisting, over-lowering, and excessive travel movement.

## Optional Features

Depending on application requirements, an Underslung EOT Crane may also be supplied with optional features such as:

- **Single Speed or Dual Speed Drive Options**  
- **Independent Moving Push Button Arrangement** for handling oversized jobs  
- **Different control station options** based on operating preference  
- **Application-specific motor selection**  
- **Additional motion and safety control arrangements** where required  

## Design and Safety Considerations

The selection of an underslung crane should consider:

- rated load and lifting frequency  
- available building support arrangement  
- required span and travel path  
- lifting height requirement  
- operating speed preference  
- control method  
- safety requirements for lifting and travel motions  

Appropriate safety integration, including braking, limit switching, and controlled operation, is important for reliable and safe use in industrial environments.

## Applications of Underslung EOT Cranes

Underslung cranes are suitable for lifting and shifting materials in:

- workshops  
- industrial units  
- light fabrication areas  
- construction-related applications  
- facilities with restricted floor or structural space  
- locations where conventional EOT crane installation is difficult  

They are commonly used where overhead movement is needed but building conditions favor a suspended crane arrangement.

## Why Choose an Underslung EOT Crane

An underslung crane is a practical solution when the application requires:

- compact overhead lifting  
- better use of available space  
- installation in constrained building conditions  
- reliable light to medium-duty material handling  
- flexible operating and speed options  

For facilities where conventional crane arrangements are difficult to accommodate, the underslung EOT crane provides an efficient and economical material handling option.
