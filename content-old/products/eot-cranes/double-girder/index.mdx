## Double Girder EOT Crane

A Double Girder EOT Crane is a heavy-duty overhead lifting system designed for applications that require higher lifting capacities, wider spans, improved hook height, and dependable performance under demanding operating conditions. It consists of two bridge girders mounted on end carriages that travel along runway rails, with the hoisting trolley or crab arrangement running on top of the girders.

This configuration is widely used in industrial facilities where heavier loads must be lifted, transported, and positioned safely across large working areas. It is commonly selected for steel plants, power plants, engineering shops, heavy fabrication units, warehouses, process plants, and outdoor material handling applications.

## Construction and Working Principle

The double girder arrangement uses two parallel girders to support the trolley or crab mechanism. This design provides higher structural strength and better stability than a single girder system, making it suitable for demanding duty cycles and higher capacities.

The crane typically performs movement in three directions:

- **Hoisting Motion:** vertical lifting and lowering of the load  
- **Cross Travel Motion:** movement of the trolley or crab across the bridge  
- **Long Travel Motion:** movement of the complete crane bridge along the runway  

This three-axis operation enables safe and controlled movement of heavy materials throughout the work area.

## Key Product Advantages

### Designed for Heavy-Duty Lifting

Double girder cranes are widely used where the application involves heavier loads, higher duty cycles, or longer spans than a single girder crane can economically handle. Their structural arrangement supports intensive industrial lifting operations with better rigidity and load distribution.

### Improved Hook Height

Because the trolley or crab operates on top of the girders, the crane can provide better hook height and more effective use of vertical space inside the building. This makes the double girder arrangement especially suitable where maximum lifting height is important.

### Better Suitability for Wider Spans

The twin-girder structure is preferred for large bays and longer runway spans. It offers improved strength and stability for transporting loads across wider working areas without compromising operating reliability.

### Suitable for Heavy Industrial Environments

Double girder EOT cranes are commonly used in:

- steel plants  
- power plants  
- cement plants  
- engineering industries  
- transformer and cable industries  
- fabrication shops  
- process plants  
- workshops and warehouses  
- storage yards and material handling areas  

## Typical Technical Specifications

Actual crane configuration depends on the application, load spectrum, building dimensions, and operating duty. Common technical parameters include:

- **Safe Working Load:** up to 100 ton  
- **Span:** generally from 5 m to 50 m  
- **Height of Lift:** selected as per application and building requirement  
- **Duty Classification:** selected based on operating frequency and load conditions  
- **Trolley Arrangement:** top running trolley or crab type mechanism  
- **Control System:** pendant push button, optional radio remote, or cabin operation  
- **Drive System:** motorized hoisting, cross travel, and long travel motions  
- **Speed Selection:** configured based on application, process requirement, and shed dimensions  

## Standard Technical Features

### Structural Design

Double girder cranes are typically built with twin bridge girders engineered for strength, rigidity, and reliable load handling. The design is selected according to capacity, span, and class of duty, with attention to safe load transfer and long-term structural performance.

### Hoist / Crab Arrangement

For higher capacities and heavy-duty applications, the crane may be equipped with a crab arrangement or other suitable hoisting mechanism designed for stable lifting and controlled load travel.

### Motors

Crane-duty squirrel cage induction motors are commonly used for hoisting, cross travel, and long travel motions. These motors are selected for frequent starting, reversing, and braking conditions encountered in industrial lifting operations.

### Brakes

Fail-safe braking systems are used to provide controlled stopping and safer load handling during regular operation as well as during power failure or emergency conditions. Braking provisions are typically applied to hoisting, cross travel, and long travel motions.

### Gear Transmission

The crane drive system generally uses geared transmission arrangements designed for smooth motion, controlled torque delivery, and durable operation under industrial working conditions.

### Electrical Panel

The electrical control panel is designed for safe power distribution, operational control, and protection of crane components. Typical provisions may include overload protection, control gear, and maintenance-friendly wiring arrangements.

### Limit Switches

Safety limit switches are a key part of the crane system and are commonly used for:

- upper hook travel limitation  
- lower hook travel limitation  
- end travel protection where required in trolley or bridge motion  

These safety provisions help reduce the risk of over-travel, over-hoisting, and accidental collision.

## Optional Features

Depending on operating needs and site conditions, a Double Girder EOT Crane may be supplied with optional features such as:

- **Variable Frequency Drive (VFD / VVVF Drive)** for smoother speed control  
- **Soft Starter** for controlled motor starting  
- **Radio Remote Control** for wireless operation  
- **Cabin Operation** for specific heavy-duty applications  
- **Shrouded DSL / Bus Bar Power Supply System**  
- **Flameproof Execution** for selected industrial environments  
- **Slip Ring Motors or other drive configurations** where required by the application  

## Design and Safety Considerations

The selection of a double girder crane should consider:

- rated load and future load growth  
- required span and runway layout  
- hook approach and lifting height  
- duty cycle and frequency of use  
- building clearance and structural constraints  
- preferred control system  
- indoor or outdoor operating conditions  

The crane should also include appropriate safety systems for load control, travel limitation, braking reliability, and electrical protection suitable to the application.

## Standards and Engineering Considerations

Double girder EOT cranes are generally engineered with reference to applicable Indian standards related to crane design, safety, structural integrity, and testing. Standards-based design is important for ensuring reliable performance, safe operation, and suitability for industrial use.

Applicable design references may include provisions related to crane structure, class of duty, drive arrangements, safety clearances, and testing requirements.
