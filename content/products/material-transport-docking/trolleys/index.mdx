## The Versatility of Unstructured Material Transport

Where pallet trucks handle **unit loads**, much of daily handling is **non-palletized**: parts, tools, drums, and mixed cartons. **Industrial trolleys** cover that gap with **steel or stainless** frames, **powder-coated** or **galvanized** finishes, and **four-wheel** layouts—often **two swivel and two rigid**, or **all swivel** with **brakes** on two—for control on ramps and docks.

**Typical catalogue bands** (non-binding): light carts from roughly **~50 kg** through **standard platform** units often quoted around **~300–1,000 kg**, and **heavy-duty** decks into **multi-tonne** ratings; **beam / gear trolleys** for runway travel may be offered into roughly **~0.5–20 t** class depending on OEM—always read the **rated capacity** for **your** wheel, frame, and application.

## Structural Typologies and Load Capacities

* **Platform (flatbed):** Open deck—often **~900–1,800 mm** long by **~600–1,200 mm** wide in common marketing sheets; **mild steel** (e.g. **14-gauge** class called out in some lines), **stainless**, or **reinforced plastic** for wet or chemical areas.
* **Cage / roll cage:** Mesh sides to contain loose cartons or parts.
* **Shelf / picking:** Multiple tiers for sortation and e-commerce style picks.
* **Tool cabinet:** Drawer stacks with **ball-bearing** slides—see **reference specification** below.
* **Drum:** Curved supports for **cylindrical** loads—often roughly **~0.35–1 t** class in catalogue examples.
* **Beam (hoist) trolley:** Travels an **I-beam** or **monorail**—**not** the same as a floor cart; capacities are **lifting-system** ratings.

## Caster Wheel Engineering

Wheel **compound** trades **roll resistance**, **noise**, **floor marking**, and **chemistry** exposure:

* **Nylon / hard tread:** Low rolling resistance, durable; harder on joints on **uneven** floors.
* **Polyurethane (PU):** Common **heavy-duty** choice—load capacity with moderate damping; many **platform** lines specify **PU** casters.
* **Rubber / non-marking:** Softer ride; may trade off maximum load vs same-size PU.

Sizes such as **~125 × 30 mm** appear often in **light–medium** platform specs; **heavy tool cabinets** may specify **larger** wheels (see reference).

The following **binding-style** items are transcribed from a **tool trolley** technical compliance sheet (**seven-drawer** industrial cabinet). They describe **one** procurement profile—not every Jayco floor trolley. Substitute **Jayco’s approved drawing** for your order.

**Construction:** Sturdy **steel**, **heavy-gauge** sheet frame with **integrated tray** compartments.

**Overall dimensions (minimum):** **Height × Length × Width** — **925 × 660 × 475 mm** (**±25 mm**).

**Drawers:**

* **Count / travel:** **Seven** drawers, **full extension**, with **locking** system.
* **Internal (minimum):** **500 × 350 mm** (**±10 mm**).
* **Panel heights:** **Five** drawers at **70 mm** (**±5 mm**); **Two** drawers at **150 mm** (**±10 mm**).
* **Slides:** Roller guide / **ball-bearing** slides for **heavy** drawer operation.
* **Load per drawer (minimum):** **20 kg** each.

**Mobility:** **Heavy-duty** castors **120 × 30 mm** (**±5 mm**): **2** swivel **+ 2** swivel **with brake**; **side handle** for maneuvering.

**Finish:** **Powder coated**.

**Commercial (tender notes):** Package bidding; **OEM** or **authorized** distributor; **catalogue/drawing** required; parameters marked **essential**; **1-year OEM warranty** cited—confirm current Jayco terms on quotation.

## Ergonomic Safety Guidelines

Heavy **push/pull** forces drive **MSD** risk—keep **loads within rating**, use **brakes** on inclines, and ensure **visibility** around the load. Match **trolley size** to **aisle** and **door** clearances; on **docks**, watch **expansion joints** and **level** changes that catch small wheels.
